UniAGORA Product Requirements Document (PRD)
 
Version: 1.0 (MVP)
 
1. Executive Summary
Product Name
 
UniAGORA
 
Vision
 
UniAGORA is a trusted multi-university marketplace that enables students to discover, buy, and sell products within their university through verified vendor profiles.
 
The platform aims to create a safe, student-focused digital marketplace by combining verified identities, trusted vendors, real-time communication, and a simple buying experience.
 
2. Product Goals
 
The MVP should allow users to:
 
Create an account
Browse products
Search for products
Contact vendors
Become verified vendors
List products
Complete transactions offline
Leave trusted reviews
Build trust through vendor verification
3. Target Users
 
The platform supports multiple universities.
 
Each user belongs to one selected university at a time.
 
Users may change their university whenever they wish.
 
4. User Roles
Customer (Default)
 
Every registered account is automatically a Customer.
 
Customers can:
 
Register
Login
Browse products
Search products
Filter products
View stores
Contact vendors
Save products
Report products
Report vendors
Leave reviews after completed transactions
Apply to become a Vendor
 
Customers may browse the marketplace immediately after registration.
 
Email verification will be introduced in a future release.
 
Vendor
 
A Customer may upgrade to a Vendor.
 
Each account may own only one vendor profile.
 
Vendor types:
 
Student Vendor
 
Required information:
 
Store Name
Phone Number
University
Matric Number (Unique)
Department
Level
 
One proof of studentship:
 
Admission Letter
Student ID Card
Course Registration Slip
School Fee Receipt
Business Vendor
 
Required:
 
Store Name
Business Name
Phone Number
Business Address
 
Optional:
 
Business Logo
 
Supporting business documents will be introduced after MVP.
 
Vendor capabilities:
 
Manage Store
Create Listings
Edit Listings
Delete Listings
Mark Products as Sold
Manage Inventory
Respond to Customer Chats
View Reviews
View Enquiries
 
Vendors retain every Customer capability.
 
Admin
 
Admin accounts are created internally.
 
Capabilities:
 
Dashboard
User Management
Vendor Management
Product Moderation
Category Management
Report Management
Marketplace Analytics
Platform Settings
5. Vendor Verification
 
For MVP:
 
Vendor applications are automatically approved after submission.
 
Verification statuses:
 
Pending
Verified
Rejected
Suspended
 
Public badges:
 
✓ Verified Student
 
✓ Verified Business
 
6. Marketplace
 
Every product belongs to exactly one Vendor.
 
Products may belong to multiple Categories.
 
Each listing contains:
 
Product Name
Description
Price
Categories
Condition
Quantity
Campus / Location
Availability
Date Listed
 
Images:
 
One primary image (required)
Up to seven additional images
 
Maximum:
 
Eight images.
 
Product conditions:
 
New
Used
 
Inventory is quantity-based.
 
When quantity reaches zero:
 
The product becomes Out of Stock automatically.
 
7. Product Lifecycle
 
Vendor creates product
 
↓
 
Visible in marketplace
 
↓
 
Customers contact vendor
 
↓
 
Transaction completed offline
 
↓
 
Vendor marks transaction as completed
 
↓
 
Customer can leave review
 
Listings expire after 30 days.
 
Expired listings:
 
Hidden from marketplace
Not deleted
Can be renewed by the Vendor

Messaging is Customer → Vendor only.
 
Customers initiate conversations.

Text Messaging
 
Optional:
 
Image Attachments (if time permits)

Customers may:
 
Save Products
Remove Products

12. Reviews
 
Customers may review only after:
 
Vendor marks the transaction as completed.
 
Reviews include:
 
Rating (1–5)
Optional Comment
 
Customers may edit their reviews.
 
13. Reports
 
Customers may report:
 
Products
Vendors
 
Reports are managed by Admin.
 
14. Notifications
 
Push notifications include:
 
New Messages
Vendor Verification Updates

New Reviews
Platform Announcements
15. Marketplace Rules
One account per email
One vendor profile per account
One unique matric number per student vendor
Products belong to one Vendor
Products may belong to multiple Categories
Vendors retain Customer permissions
Admin may suspend Vendors
Admin may remove listings
Listings expire after 30 days
Vendors may renew expired listings
Vendor marks completed transactions
Customers may review completed transactions only

17. API Principles
 
The backend is API-first.
 
The same REST API serves:
 
Web
Mobile
Admin
 
Every endpoint follows a consistent response structure:
 
Success:
 
{
  "success": true,
  "message": "",
  "data": {}
}
 
Failure:
 
{
  "success": false,
  "message": "",
  "errors": {}
}
 
API versioning begins at:
 
/api/v1/
 
The backend owns all business rules. Client applications must never implement business logic.
